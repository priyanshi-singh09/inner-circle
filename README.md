# Inner Circle

A social platform built around honest self-expression, emotional discovery, human connection, and empathy over popularity.

## Vision

A place where you can be yourself without performing for anyone.

## Initial Product Areas

- Express thoughts with a handle or anonymously
- Discover posts by emotion
- Connect through following and mutual connections
- Support people through empathetic reactions and comments
- Circle-based experiences with age-appropriate safety

## Project Status

Early foundation — product and technical architecture are being built incrementally.
