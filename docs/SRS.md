# Inner Circle — Software Requirements Specification

## 1. Product Vision

Inner Circle is a social platform for honest self-expression, emotional discovery, connection, and empathy over popularity.

## 2. Core Principles

- Share without fear.
- Listen without judgment.
- Support without comparison.
- Give new voices opportunities to be discovered.

## 3. V1 Features

- Create a thought
- Post anonymously or with a custom handle
- Emotion selection
- Circle-based content discovery
- Feed
- Explore: posts, people, related topics
- Empathetic reactions: I Hear You, Relate, Rooting For You
- Supportive comments
- Following
- Mutual connections
- Mutual-connection messaging
- Notifications
- Report and block
- Moderation and age-appropriate safety controls

## 4. Age Circles

- Firefly Circle: 13–15
- Night Owl Circle: 16–18
- Wildflower Circle: 19–22
- Compass Circle: 23–27
- Anchor Grove: 28–35
- Evergreen Circle: 36–50
- Lighthouse Circle: 51+

Circle assignment is derived from age rather than manually selected by the user.

## 5. V1 Non-Goals

Stories, Reels, live streaming, public follower rankings, advertising, marketplace features, gamification, complex AI recommendation systems, and advanced creator tools are intentionally deferred.
